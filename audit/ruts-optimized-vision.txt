## Overall verdict

All captures read as dry, matte dirt roads. No broad painted stripe, tar-like sheen, obvious texture banding, or visible cracking appears. Fine road geometry does not introduce visible seams.

The wheel ruts are most legible on cabinTrail, moderately visible on stage, and largely indeterminate on townMain because the views show a broad street/courtyard and are partially occluded.

### stage

- **Eye/detail, midday:** Broad pale dirt surface with subtle darker wheel-track depressions and irregular wear. Matte response; no stripe or sheen.
- **Eye/detail, golden:** Same road structure under warm lighting. The pink-brown tint is consistent with illumination, not a painted band.
- **Geometry:** No tiling seams or hard transitions visible.
- **Metadata:** Approximately 563–569 draws, 6.69–6.71M tris, 47–50 textures, 163–227MB. No runtime/WebGPU error text visible.

### townMain

- **Eye, midday:** Road surface reads as dry dirt, though the central foreground is dominated by a large dark vehicle/object and the wheel-rut structure is not assessable.
- **Detail, midday:** Street surface appears mostly broad and flat, with natural mottling rather than two clearly recessed ruts.
- **Eye/detail, golden:** Warm lighting changes the dirt toward mauve-brown but does not create a stripe or reflective coating. Ruts remain weak or obscured.
- **Geometry:** No visible seams, cracks, or repeating bands.
- **Metadata:** Approximately 315–330 draws, 6.71–6.72M tris, 44–47 textures, 163–227MB. No runtime/WebGPU error text visible.

### cabinTrail

- **Eye/detail, midday:** Clearest road example. Two darker longitudinal wheel paths and a lighter raised center/shoulder area give the road a convincing recessed-rut read.
- **Eye/detail, golden:** Rut shading remains visible and coherent under warm light. The surface stays matte; no tar sheen or painted strip appears.
- **Geometry:** No visible seams across the fine road geometry. The irregular grass boundary is visible but does not expose road mesh tiling.
- **Metadata:** Approximately 235–274 draws, 7.13–7.16M tris, 44–47 textures, 163–227MB. No runtime/WebGPU error text visible.

Performance overlays do show severe golden-light frame-rate drops in detail views—especially townMain and cabinTrail—but these are performance readings, not visible runtime/WebGPU errors.
