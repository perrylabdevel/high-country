VISION_ANALYSIS

1. The road shows a convincing longitudinal two-groove rut pattern with a raised central crown, especially in the eye-level frames. The depression is subtle but reads as geometry rather than only colour variation.

2. Noticeable horizontal/longitudinal banding remains across the rut floors and some edges under both midday and golden lighting. It appears as repetitive fine striping/texture interference, strongest in the foreground detail views.

3. The material reads predominantly dry and matte. Surface normals provide useful relief, but the repeated patterned response makes them look somewhat synthetic rather than fully natural.

4. No clear tar sheen or painted road stripes are visible. Dark tracks appear to be dirt/rut shading and relief, not glossy asphalt or markings.
