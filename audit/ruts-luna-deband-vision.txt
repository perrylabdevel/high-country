- Two-groove geometry: Shared depressions are visible mainly as broad, shallow longitudinal tracks; the paired ruts read credibly without excessive fragment relief. Groove separation is subtle but coherent.
- Crown: The road retains a readable raised center/crown, especially toward the midground. The crown is softened and broad rather than sharply peaked.
- Banding: Longitudinal tonal banding is present in both frames, following the road direction. Midday shows restrained, natural variation; golden light increases warm contrast and makes the bands more apparent without producing obvious striping.
- Material: The surface reads dry, matte, and dusty. Highlights remain diffuse, with no wet sheen. Golden lighting warms the road substantially but does not make it glossy.
- Overall: The 5 cm relief successfully reduces distracting micro-rut harshness while preserving the physical heightfield’s road structure.
