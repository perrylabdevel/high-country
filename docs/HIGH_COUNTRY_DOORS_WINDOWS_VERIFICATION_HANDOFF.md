Perform a complete, evidence-backed verification and repair campaign for **every door and every window in the High Country repository and runnable world**.

This is not a representative sample and not a visual spot-check. Build a complete inventory, verify every discovered aperture and associated component, repair defects within scope, and leave behind automated checks that prevent regressions.

The runnable game is the source of truth. Do not declare success from code inspection, compilation, primitive counts, or a few attractive screenshots.

## Door verification

Verify every door against all applicable categories below.

### Geometry and scale

- Human-scale width and height appropriate to its building and purpose.
- Door leaf fits its framed opening with believable clearance.
- Frame, jamb, lintel, threshold, trim, and wall opening align.
- No floating, sinking, wall penetration, detached trim, inverted faces, z-fighting, or light-visible gaps that should not exist.
- Steps, porch, and threshold meet terrain and floor elevations correctly.
- Double doors, gates, barn doors, saloon doors, and other special forms have appropriate proportions and pivots.

Do not enforce one universal dimension blindly. Validate against declared type-specific ranges and human scale. Any intentional exception must be explicitly classified and justified.

### Exterior/interior consistency

- Exterior and interior door positions, sizes, orientation, and state agree.
- A visible exterior door opens into valid interior space or is explicitly classified as inaccessible/decorative.
- Accessible interiors do not contain missing, offset, duplicated, or contradictory doorways.
- The wall opening remains open when the door leaf is open; invisible wall geometry or colliders must not continue blocking it.

### Interaction and state

- Interaction prompts appear only within the correct range and on the correct side or sides.
- Open, close, locked, unlocked, boarded, and scripted states behave as declared.
- Animation pivots and swing direction are believable and do not drive the leaf through walls, furniture, terrain, or the player.
- State persists correctly through save/load when persistence is intended.
- Rapid or repeated interaction cannot duplicate leaves, desynchronize collision, or trap the player.
- Decorative doors do not falsely advertise enterability; visually convincing entrances that should work must become defects rather than being relabeled decorative for convenience.

### Collision and traversal

- Closed solid doors block the player correctly.
- Open traversable doors allow the real player capsule through with reasonable clearance.
- Door frames block where geometry exists without creating oversized invisible barriers.
- Approaching from both sides works when the space is accessible from both sides.
- Opening or closing near the player cannot permanently trap, launch, or embed the player.
- Mounted traversal is tested only where the doorway is intended to support it.

Use collision sweeps or deterministic geometry checks for every door. Use real-player navigation for every traversable door when feasible and for all distinct door implementations at minimum.

Automated player movement must use closed-loop navigation: orient toward short waypoints, apply brief movement pulses, measure displacement, detect obstruction, recover, replan, and fail within bounded time. Never hold forward indefinitely against a wall, door, frame, or prop. Do not teleport, disable collisions, or phase through geometry to claim a traversal pass.

## Window verification

Verify every window against all applicable categories below.

### Geometry and placement

- Human-scale width, height, sill height, and spacing appropriate to the building type.
- Window opening, frame, sill, lintel, trim, glazing, shutters, and wall thickness align.
- No floating frames, buried sills, detached shutters, doubled surfaces, z-fighting, inverted normals, light leaks, or glass outside its opening.
- Windows do not intersect roofs, floors, corners, doors, chimneys, porches, furniture, or terrain.
- Repeated windows follow deliberate architectural rhythm without obvious accidental cloning.
- Upper-storey windows actually fit inside upper-storey walls and align with believable floors.

### Exterior/interior consistency

- Exterior windows correspond to valid interior wall positions when interiors exist.
- Interior and exterior views agree on aperture location, approximate size, height, and blocked/open state.
- A window does not show daylight into solid interior geometry or reveal missing world surfaces unintentionally.
- Boarded, curtained, shuttered, broken, opaque, and inaccessible windows are explicitly classified and represented consistently on both sides when both sides exist.

### Materials, lighting, and visibility

- Glass is visibly distinct from an empty hole and from an opaque painted panel.
- Glazing behaves acceptably in midday and golden-hour lighting without becoming pure black, fully invisible, mirror-like inappropriately, or excessively bright.
- Transparency order, depth behavior, reflections, tint, roughness, dirt, and opacity remain coherent under the target WebGPU renderer.
- Frames and trim remain readable at player distance and do not collapse into wall texture.
- Interior darkness behind a window is believable and does not look like a rendering failure.
- Repeated panes and reflections do not create distracting moiré, flicker, or obvious tiling.

### Gameplay and collision

- Solid windows and surrounding walls block the player and camera as intended.
- Broken or deliberately traversable windows behave according to their classification.
- Interaction prompts exist only for windows designed to support interaction.
- Windows do not snag the camera, allow unintended peeking through solid geometry, or create collision volumes larger than their visible construction.

## Verification strategy

Use the cheapest reliable instrument for each fact, but every inventory item must receive a final status.

### Deterministic checks for every item

Compute and validate:

- transforms, dimensions, orientation, wall containment, and floor/terrain relationship;
- overlap and intersection against doors, windows, corners, roofs, floors, and known props;
- opening-to-frame and exterior-to-interior correspondence;
- collider state versus declared open/closed/traversable state;
- player-capsule clearance for traversable doors;
- duplicate, missing, orphaned, and unclassified components;
- finite values and valid geometry/material references.

Prove important checks can fail by temporarily injecting at least one known historical or synthetic defect in a controlled test, observing the failure, and restoring the correct implementation. Do not commit the injected defect.

### Visual inspection for every item

Create an automated aperture-inspection capture mode that frames every door and window consistently. It may use a debug camera because this is visual inspection, not a travel test.

For each aperture, collect sufficient views for its classification:

- exterior front or slight-angle view;
- interior counterpart when an accessible interior exists;
- open and closed views for moving doors;
- a close view only when needed to diagnose frame, glass, collision, or material defects.

Use compact contact sheets or indexed atlases for efficient first-pass review, but preserve enough resolution to inspect defects. A vision critic may review batches, yet every aperture ID must receive an explicit result. Re-run focused before/after captures only for failures and repairs; do not run the unrelated full-world screenshot rubric.

Verify representative windows under both midday and golden-hour lighting, and expand to all windows sharing a material/renderer path if a lighting-dependent defect appears.

### Functional verification

- Exercise every distinct door implementation and state transition.
- Traverse every door declared traversable, or provide deterministic per-door capsule-sweep evidence plus real-player traversal for each distinct implementation when world-scale traversal cost would be disproportionate.
- Navigate real representative routes through exterior entrance, interior doorway, and exit sequences.
- Test interaction from both sides where applicable.
- Test save/load state for every door class intended to persist.
- Test representative window collision and camera behavior for every distinct implementation class, expanding to individual instances when deterministic checks find variance.

No test may hang indefinitely. Apply explicit per-door movement, recovery, and interaction timeouts and report failures diagnostically.

## Repair policy

- Fix shared generators before patching individual instances when the generator is the cause.
- Do not force unique architecture through an inappropriate shared abstraction.
- Preserve building identity and period-appropriate variation.
- Do not solve mismatches by deleting windows, sealing doorways, weakening colliders, hiding defects with dark materials, or relabeling expected entrances as decorative.
- Do not weaken a valid check to make current geometry pass.
- Keep each repair scoped and rerun the affected deterministic, functional, and visual evidence.
- Check nearby building surfaces for regressions after shared-kit changes.
- Revert failed speculative fixes rather than stacking patches.

## Acceptance gates

Do not declare the campaign complete until all of the following are true:

- Every runtime and source-discovered door and window has a stable inventory ID and declared classification.
- There are zero unexplained differences between declared, constructed, and runtime aperture counts.
- Every inventory item has deterministic verification results.
- Every inventory item has an explicit visual-review status.
- Every traversable door has direct traversal evidence or an explicitly documented combination of deterministic clearance evidence and representative real-player traversal for its implementation class.
- All distinct door interaction/state implementations have been exercised.
- Exterior/interior correspondence passes wherever both sides exist.
- There are zero unexplained floating, buried, detached, intersecting, duplicated, blocked, or unclassified apertures.
- There are zero unbounded navigation or interaction hangs.
- Build and existing repository checks pass.
- Targeted regression checks pass after shared-generator repairs.
- Remaining failures are genuine external blockers, not deferred ordinary defects.

“Mostly verified,” “representative buildings look correct,” and “all generated instances should share the fix” are not acceptable conclusions.
